# Product

## Register

product

## Users

**Adopters** — individuals looking to adopt a pet. They're in an emotional, high-stakes decision process: they want to find the right companion and feel confident the adoption will succeed. Primary tasks: browsing pets, submitting applications, communicating with shelters, completing onboarding to improve matches.

**Shelter staff** — rescue organizations managing adoptions end-to-end. They need efficient tools to list pets, review applications, communicate with adopters, and track outcomes. Primary tasks: managing pet listings, reviewing and approving applications, coordinating with team members, configuring adoption policies.

## Product Purpose

Tovitu reduces failed adoptions by helping the right pets find the right people. Through AI-powered compatibility analysis, life previews of pets in an adopter's environment, and structured support before and after adoption, the platform makes matches that stick. Success means fewer pets returned and more successful, lasting adoptions.

## Brand Personality

Playful, bold, friendly. Tovitu brings warmth and confidence to a process that can feel heavy. The platform should feel like a capable, cheerful guide — never corporate or sterile, never overly sentimental. Color and conviction carry the warmth.

## Anti-references

- Corporate / serious / B2B. Tovitu is not a bank, a legal tool, or enterprise software. No navy suits, no muted professionalism.
- Generic AI/SaaS design vocabulary: cream backgrounds, glass cards, gradient text, uniform card grids with icon + heading + body.
- Overly cute or childish — pets are joyful but adoption is serious; the brand should carry both.

## Design Principles

1. **Bold clarity.** Every interaction is unambiguous. Thick borders, clear labels, oversized targets. Nothing hides behind polish.
2. **Playful confidence.** The platform feels joyful and approachable. Color and typography carry the energy — not decoration or ornament.
3. **Function over decoration.** No ornamental blurs, gradients, or glass effects. Every visual element has a job. If it doesn't help the user make a decision, remove it.
4. **Accessibility as a feature.** High contrast and large targets are the look, not a compromise. WCAG AA is the baseline, not the ceiling.
5. **Trust through transparency.** Adoption is emotional and high-stakes. Clear information hierarchy, honest copy, and predictable interactions build confidence in every step.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance target. Keyboard navigation, screen reader support, 4.5:1 minimum contrast for body text, 3:1 for large text. Reduced motion support required. Multi-language support in English and Spanish throughout.
